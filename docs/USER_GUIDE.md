# User Guide — Leftover Chef

## 1. Finding Recipes with Your Leftover Ingredients
1. Open the **Match Ingredients** view on the Home Page.
2. Type one or more ingredients separated by commas (for example: `potato, onion, tomato, rice, cheese`) and click **Add Ingredients**.
3. Your entry is automatically converted into individual removable ingredient chips. You can also click any **Suggested Staple** chip or **Use Pantry** to import your saved pantry items.
4. Optional: Filter by **Dietary Preference** (`Vegetarian`, `Vegan`, `Gluten-Free`, `Dairy-Free`, `Nut-Free`), **Difficulty**, **Max Total Time**, or **Exclude Ingredients** (`e.g. peanuts, milk`), and choose a sort order (`Best Ingredient Match`, `Fewest Missing Ingredients`, `Shortest Total Cooking Time`, `Alphabetical`).
5. Review the ranked recipe cards. Each card shows:
   - Exact **Required Match %**
   - **Matched Required** vs **Missing Required** counts
   - Which ingredients you **Have** and which you **Need**

## 2. Viewing Recipe Details & Adjusting Servings
1. Click **View Recipe** on any card.
2. Use the **`-` / `+` Servings** buttons to scale ingredient quantities dynamically (from 1 to 50 servings). Qualitative amounts like `"to taste"` are preserved automatically.
3. Click **Add Missing to List** to send all missing required ingredients directly to your Shopping List.
4. Follow the numbered **Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions** and review the **Allergen & Food Safety Precautions** banner.

## 3. Managing Your Pantry, Shopping List & Favourites
- **My Pantry**: Add ingredients you keep at home with quantities, units, optional best-before dates, and storage notes. Click **Search Recipes Using Pantry** to match recipes immediately.
- **Shopping List**: Review missing ingredients, check off purchased items, and click **Clear Purchased Items**. Compatible units (`g` + `g`) merge automatically while incompatible units (`g` vs `cup`) stay separate.
- **Favourites**: Click the heart icon on any recipe card or modal to save it for quick access.
